# Your knowledge, within reach

This is the **Mementara starter vault**: a small notebook you can copy, explore, and read offline.

## Try it in two minutes

1. Open [[Guides/Getting started|Getting started]].
2. Search for **compass** to find a specific passage.
3. Bookmark the passage, close the app, and reopen it offline.
4. Follow [a relative link](Guides/Getting%20started.md#your-first-vault).

> [!TIP]
> Your GitHub repository stays the source of truth. Mementara is a read-only companion.

## A notebook that travels

A compass is useful even when the network disappears. Your downloaded notes work the same way. ^compass
