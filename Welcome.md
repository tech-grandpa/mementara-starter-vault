# Your knowledge, within reach

![A quiet hillside under connected stars](Assets/field-notes.svg)

Welcome to a small **observatory of ideas**. There is a journal to read, a sketch to explore, and a handful of connections waiting to be found.

**Start here: [[Guides/Demo tour|take the five-minute tour]].**

## Try it in two minutes

1. Search for **compass** to find a remembered passage.
2. Bookmark it, close the app, and reopen it offline.
3. Open [the illustrated field journal](Formats/Astronomy.pdf).
4. Browse [[Formats/Overview.markdown|the format gallery]] for diagrams, a canvas and reading notes.

> [!TIP]
> In the content-aware version 2 prototype, search can look inside PDF text, diagram labels and text exports. The demo tour gives you a few words to try.

## A notebook that travels

A compass is useful even when the network disappears. Your downloaded notes work the same way. ^compass

An idea might begin in a sketch, become a paragraph, and end up in a checklist. Keep those pieces together and leave a small trail between them.
