# A five-minute demo tour

You are planning a quiet evening outside. A few reading notes, a sketch and a field journal are all you need. Follow this small project through the different ways a second brain can hold an idea.

## 1. Read a page worth keeping

Open [the illustrated field journal](../Formats/Astronomy.pdf). Its three short pages move from a simple plan, to an observation, to a reflection.

Back in the library, search for **Andromeda discovery**. The content-aware prototype finds the text on **page two**, even though those words are not in the filename.

## 2. See the connections

Open [Research.drawio](../Formats/Research.drawio). The first page connects a question with reading notes. Use the page selector to explore the second page, then switch to **Text** to read the labels.

Search for **Nebula** to find this drawing by its contents. The preview is a simplified overview; the original file remains available through Share.

## 3. Spread the project out

Open [Project.canvas](../Formats/Project.canvas) for a spatial view of the same work: a question, a journal, a reading list and a next step. Search for **Orbital** to find a card.

Then open [Sketch.excalidraw](../Formats/Sketch.excalidraw). It keeps a rough idea close to the project. Search for **Pulsar**, or switch to Text to read the drawing's labels.

## 4. Find a detail in the supporting files

| Search for | Where it leads |
| --- | --- |
| `Magnetosphere` | A topic in [Reading list.csv](../Formats/Reading%20list.csv) |
| `Heliosphere` | The saved text in [Clipping.html](../Formats/Clipping.html) |
| `Twilight` | A session entry in [Evening plan.yaml](../Formats/Evening%20plan.yaml) |
| `Lenticular` | A tag in [Observation log.json](../Formats/Observation%20log.json) |

These are small, readable exports. The app searches their text without running the source or contacting a rendering service.

## 5. Bring the original along

Open [Evening checklist.docx](../Formats/Evening%20checklist.docx) or [Rich note.rtf](../Formats/Rich%20note.rtf), then choose **Open system preview**. Use Share to open the original in another app.

[Evening reminder.m4a](../Formats/Evening%20reminder.m4a) is a short, synthetic spoken note. Its transcript is in [[Media#A short spoken note|Media]]. Audio and Office files are searchable by **filename**; their contents are not indexed or transcribed.

## Take it offline

- [ ] Wait for the sample download to finish.
- [ ] Bookmark the compass passage in [[Welcome]].
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode and reopen the app.
- [ ] Search a different word and open the downloaded file.

On iPad, leave the library beside your document as you follow the tour. On iPhone, use Back to return to the results.

The richer previews and content search need the content-aware version 2 development build. Textless scans need OCR, which this prototype does not include.
